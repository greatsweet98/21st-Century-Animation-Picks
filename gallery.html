21st Century Animation Picks: Series, Franchises, & Pilots That Made Us Appreciate Moving Art Throughout The Millenium and Modern Age

According to the given information I've put in my introductory and "animation influences" pages of this website of mine, here's a whole list I made containing past and present animated series, program franchises, & pilots, on international cable networks, streaming services, & the internet that are certified representatives, good examples, & "worthy recommendations" on how 21st century animation is as relevant, influential, & game-changing to pop culture, and how most or all of them should be as mainstream as "Japanese anime programs", video games, & films even nowadays. The animated programs in this list are all debuted and released in any year in the 21st century as of now since year 2000. Note that the given ones in the list is not always objective or factual at any certain point, this is just my personal opinion on what shows deserve to be recognized. If any of your favorite shows are not in the list, I treated them as honorable mentions, so that your opinions and views would still be valid. For metaphor and exaggeration, I officially marked them as the "inductees" of this hall-of-fame list I've made.

For you to have an idea on how these animated shows once or always got "relevant" or popular, the criteria within their typical cultural impact on fandoms are as follows:

  • Tumblr-favorite and fandom-coded series
  • Considered "childhood" favorites
  • Series with underrated, niche, & "deserved-to-be-heard" fanbases
  • Series with large-following fanbases (sometimes causing toxicity such as ship wars)
